PDF Study Assistant

Mindora's PDF workflow helps you study supported documents by working with selectable text and turning material into revision-friendly notes.

How to get better results

  1. Choose a PDF whose text can be selected and copied.
  2. Check that extracted text is readable and complete.
  3. Review the generated guide against the original document.

Known limitation

Scanned PDFs containing only page images may not work unless OCR support is added. AI results can contain errors, so verify facts, formulas, and quotations.
