About BattleCore

BattleCore is an independent community website offering Free Fire-related player tools and simple gaming utilities. We aim to make the tools easy to use on mobile and desktop.

Player lookup results depend on the availability, accuracy and rules of the connected third-party services. We cannot guarantee that every request will work.
