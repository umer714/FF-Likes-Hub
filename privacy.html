Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This page explains how the current Mindora website handles information. It should be updated whenever the site's data practices change.

Information stored on your device

Study goals, streaks, progress history, and saved study items may be stored in your browser using local storage. This information stays in that browser on that device unless you export it, clear browser data, or a feature is changed to use a server. It may not automatically sync between devices.

AI study tools

When you submit text or a study request to an AI-powered feature, the submitted content is sent to the website's server endpoint for processing and may be sent to the AI service configured by the site operator. Avoid submitting passwords, private records, or information you do not have permission to share. Review the AI provider's privacy terms for its handling of requests.

PDFs and study content

PDF features may process selectable text from a file you choose. Do not use confidential documents unless you understand where content is processed and have permission to use it.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The frontend uses browser storage for local study features. Hosting providers, server logs, or third-party services may process technical data. Any analytics, advertising, or additional tracking added in the future should be disclosed here before being enabled.

Your choices

  • Clear site data in your browser to remove locally stored study information.
  • Do not submit sensitive text to AI tools.
  • Keep any exported backups secure.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the Contact page. Do not post personal information publicly in issue reports.

This page is general information and is not legal advice.
