Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy describes information that may be processed when you use BattleCore.

Information you provide

If you create an account, your email address and authentication-related data are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase. We do not ask for your Free Fire password. Do not enter passwords or verification codes into player-tool forms.

Player tool requests

If you use player tools, the UID and region you submit may be sent to our server and relevant third-party service to process your request. Avoid submitting information that is not required for the tool.

Cookies and storage

Authentication services may use browser storage or similar technologies to keep you signed in and protect sessions. Hosting and service providers may process technical information needed to operate and secure their services.

Third-party services

BattleCore may rely on third-party hosting, authentication, or game-related APIs. Their processing is governed by their own policies and terms. We do not control their availability or practices.

Data security and retention

We use third-party services to operate the site, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Data may be retained by service providers according to their configurations and policies.

Children and privacy requests

Do not submit sensitive personal information. For questions or requests concerning information you have provided, contact the website team through our Contact page.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as the website changes. Continued use after an update means you can review the latest version here.
