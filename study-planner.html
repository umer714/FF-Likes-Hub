Study Planner & Progress Tracker

A practical study plan makes it easier to decide what to work on next. Mindora provides study-planning tools and a dashboard for reviewing goals and progress.

Build a simple routine

  1. Choose one or two clear topics for your next session.
  2. Set a realistic daily study goal.
  3. Study in focused sessions and take breaks as needed.
  4. Review weekly progress and adjust your plan.

Your progress data

Some progress indicators are stored locally in your browser. They may not sync across devices, and clearing browser data can remove them.
